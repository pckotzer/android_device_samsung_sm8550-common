    sm8550-common: audio: Disable compressed media offload
    
    Compressed offload opens on the Samsung OSS audio stack, but the graph
    does not expose the module tag expected by PAL during pause, flush and
    resume. Playback can stay silent until AudioFlinger falls back to
    deep-buffer.
    
    Stop advertising the broken compressed offload profile and use the
    working deep-buffer path for media playback.
